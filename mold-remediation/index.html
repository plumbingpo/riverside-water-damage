Maintenance Tips for Mold Remediation

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Mold can get going after just 24 to 48 hours of dampness, so have leaks fixed within 24 hours
  • Do not leave damp laundry, bath mats or spilled water sitting for long
  • Address condensation on windows, pipes and cold surfaces promptly
  • Clear gutters and downspouts regularly, which keeps rain from soaking the foundation walls
  • In bathrooms, basements and other damp areas, choose paint and drywall rated mold-resistant
  • Check that exhaust fans in the kitchen, laundry room and bathrooms move enough air

How to Tell You Need Mold Remediation

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Mold or mildew that comes back soon after you clean it
  • Signs of concealed moisture such as paint that bubbles, wallpaper that peels or materials that warp
  • Fuzzy or spotted growth on stored boxes, clothing or the backs of furniture
  • Headaches, allergy flare-ups or breathing symptoms that ease whenever you leave the house
  • Black, green, white or other colored growth you can see on surfaces in the building
  • A slow leak under a sink, behind an appliance or in the roof that went unnoticed
  • Persistent musty or earthy odors, especially in enclosed or humid areas

What a Professional Brings to Mold Remediation

Removing mold is work homeowners should not attempt on their own. Without containment, scrubbing or tearing out moldy material releases spores by the millions, potentially spreading contamination through the house and making the health exposure worse than it was. Capturing and containing the spores during removal takes HEPA-filtered scrubbers, protective equipment for the crew and containment kept under negative air pressure, which is how professionals do it.

A professional job goes after the moisture source too, because that is why the mold grew in the first place. Removing visible mold without correcting the underlying moisture problem means the mold will return, often within weeks. Certified remediation technicians have the training and equipment to identify hidden moisture sources, dry affected areas properly and put solutions in place that prevent recurrence.
