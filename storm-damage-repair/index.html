How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season
  • Take dated photos of the house every year, since insurers ask about prior condition
  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe

Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • From ground level, the roof shows shingles torn off, broken or raised
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • A musty smell indoors in the days after heavy rain
  • Standing water in attic spaces, especially by skylights and chimneys
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward

Why Storm Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

The repairs a storm leaves behind are tangled together, and sorting them out correctly calls for an experienced restoration crew. What seems like a small leak indoors can hide widespread water damage in wall cavities, roof decking and insulation, which turns into mold and structural decay unless it is identified and dried properly. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the route water took and find all the areas it reached.

Temporary protection such as tarps and board-up needs correct technique or it will not hold. If a tarp is fastened poorly, it can direct water where you do not want it or give out when the wind picks up. Our crews are experienced in storm damage mitigation and use the right materials and methods to give reliable temporary protection while permanent repairs are scheduled.
