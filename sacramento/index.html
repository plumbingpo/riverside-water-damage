Where Water Damage Starts in Sacramento Properties

In Sacramento, the same handful of problems accounts for nearly all the water damage we see. If you can spot these early, you have a good chance of stopping the damage before it spreads.

Pipes: When water freezes inside a line it can crack the metal or plastic, then pour out as soon as things warm up. Depending on the season, the plumbing most at risk runs through outside walls, attics and garages without heat. In older homes, aging joints and tired connectors sometimes fail with no cold snap involved. Shut off the main valve right away, because an open line can soak several rooms quickly.

Appliances: Washing machines, dishwashers and refrigerator ice makers all depend on small hoses that wear out. A water heater near the end of its life can leak from the tank bottom or fail all at once. We see many of these in Sacramento homes where the unit sits upstairs or over a finished room.

Overflows: A clogged toilet or a sink left running can put water on the floor in minutes. The water often runs under baseboards and through the ceiling of the room below. If the overflow came from the toilet bowl or a drain, treat it as contaminated and keep children and pets away.

Roof: Missing shingles, cracked flashing and clogged gutters let rain into the attic. By the time a brown ring appears overhead, the attic insulation has often been wet for a while. The crew from Riverside Restoration can dry the attic and ceiling, but the roof itself needs a repair to stop the leak.

Basement: In homes that have a basement, groundwater pushes through foundation cracks and floor joints when the soil is saturated. If the sump pump sticks or the electricity goes out mid-storm, the pit fills and spills across the slab. Finished basements suffer most, since carpet, drywall and framing all soak up water from the bottom.

Mold: Damp drywall, wood and carpet can begin to grow mold in as little as 24 to 48 hours. Many mold jobs we handle in Sacramento began with an old leak that was wiped up but never dried inside the wall. Have it looked at if you notice a stale odor, bubbling paint or dark specks around an earlier leak.

What you do before help reaches your Sacramento address has a real effect on how the job goes. Safety comes before saving anything.
