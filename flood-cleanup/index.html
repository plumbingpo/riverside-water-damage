Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. Riverside Restoration handles emergency flood cleanup across Riverside and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. With the standing water gone, our IICRC-certified technicians check the contamination level and apply the right sanitizing steps, including antimicrobial treatment, HEPA air filtration and disinfection of every affected surface. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

How to Tell You Need Flood Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Tide marks on drywall or trim where the water peaked
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • Debris in a gutter or storm drain blocks proper flow, so clean them out regularly
  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
