Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. Riverside Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout Riverside using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Our IICRC-certified technicians use moisture detection equipment to identify water that has migrated into hidden areas: behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, under cabinets and beneath flooring materials. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Shelving in low areas keeps belongings out of the water if the floor floods

  • Pour water into the sump pit a few times a year to confirm the pump kicks on
  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early

How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • Ceilings, light fixtures or walls that are actively leaking water
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Active leaks from plumbing, appliances or HVAC systems
  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets
  • Carpeting so wet it squelches when you step on it or has puddles on top
  • Standing water on any floor surface, whatever the source

The Benefits of Professional Water Extraction

How fast and how completely water is extracted decides how much of your property is damaged and what restoration will cost. A shop vacuum might need a whole day for what our truck-mounted equipment finishes in minutes, because professional extraction outpaces consumer gear hundreds of times over. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.
